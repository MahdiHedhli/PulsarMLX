The accepted source theorem remains conditional on selected compiler/lowering;
no source-error envelope replaces a_j. Independent original R1 is mandatory.
Old posthoc selected_execute is insufficient; use an additive separate staged
source/mock API. No migration of consumed attempts; no old contract mutation.

refuses. Shared Q avoids lane LCM growth. CPU/mock tests cannot establish native
