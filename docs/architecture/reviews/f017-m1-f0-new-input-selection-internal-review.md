# F017 M1-F0 New-Input Selection Internal Review

## Scope

This review covers only checkpoint-free ledger reconstruction, fixture-family
precommitment, planning estimation, analytical-retention extensibility, and the
future decoded-tensor reuse design. It does not authorize checkpoint access,
route discovery, Q6_K qualification, M1-F, or P1.

## Findings

- The first fixture remains accepted oracle evidence and is retired only from
  production route freezing. The route-stability contract and `S >= 4.0`
  threshold are unchanged.
- The complete evidence-derived payload ledger reconciles to 45. The prior 37
  was a narrower Q5_K/M1-F0/recovery subtotal and omitted eight earlier F017
  payload reads. Historical evidence was not rewritten.
- Seeds 17017007 through 17017014 and every fixture byte are frozen before any
  new real access. Selection is the first qualifying ordinal, while execution
  must evaluate and bank the full ladder.
- The estimator contract was frozen before the official simulation and uses
  only already-banked probabilities, bias, and error bounds. It is explicitly
  planning evidence, not route qualification.
- One million deterministic samples produced zero `S >= 4.0` qualifiers. The
  95% upper per-fixture rate is 0.00000384145 and the corresponding optimistic
  eight-fixture success probability is 0.0000307311, far below the pre-frozen
  0.9 planning threshold.
- The decoded-tensor reuse plan is bounded to one immutable 12-payload package,
  but it remains a future design and is not authorized by this review.
- Analytical-retention v2 is phase-extensible and rejects omitted declared
  values. The generic expert-slice formula is checkpoint-free and route
  independent. Dispatch work was not advanced after the family hard stop.

## Verdict

`NO-GO`

The proposed random-normal continuation family is not adequately powered for a
bounded real ladder under the unchanged stability contract. The correct next
step is independent adversarial review of this negative result, followed by a
checkpoint-independent fixture-family redesign or tighter pre-candidate error
research. No real access should be authorized from this package.
