## 1. Independent reconstruction — what verified

**V6 defect, reproduced from source, not taken on assertion.** `execute_f017_corrected_oracle_event_v6.py` (sha256 `89a1881e…`, matching the banked reproduction claim) accepts `checkpoint_identity` as a CLI argument at line 182 and forwards it verbatim to the consumer subprocess at line 147 — it never opens or hashes a shard. Meanwhile both target sources require that identity artifact before source construction (`os.open(identity_file, …)`, primary L64 / secondary L54) and then **independently reopen graph shards by path** (`os.open(self.root / shard, …)`, primary L116 / secondary L100) reading via `os.pread` (L139 / L114). Every field of the reproduction artifact is confirmed: producer absent, `checkpoint_shard_open_operations: 0`, `checkpoint_shard_hash_operations: 0`, `graph_shards_reopened_by_path: true`, `io_mode: PREAD`.

**Attack lifecycle ordering.** The V6 exposure is two-sided and V7 closes both sides. (a) The identity artifact was an *externally supplied path* — a forged or stale identity manifest was accepted on the consumer's word; V7 sets `external_checkpoint_identity_path_permitted: false` and makes the coordinator the producer. (b) Even with a valid identity, the bind-to-read gap was open: whatever was hashed and what was later `pread` were resolved by two independent `open()` calls, so a swap between authorization and read went undetected. V7 closes this by hashing and reading through **the same open file description** — `SUBPROCESS_PASS_FDS_EXPLICIT`, `path_reopen_permitted: false`, `FSTAT_MATCHES_IDENTITY_MANIFEST`. Because the inherited fd pins the inode, the classic TOCTOU is structurally eliminated rather than detected after the fact. `durable_identity_uses_raw_fd_number: false` correctly refuses to treat fd numbers as durable identity.

**Six identity hashes, expected byte count, identity-only closure — all reconcile exactly.** The catalog gives the decisive independent confirmation: shard `00001-of-00006` carries `n_tensors: 0`, with `data_section_start == file_size_bytes == 9,423,744`, and **no tensor in the 1809-tensor census references it**. The remaining 1809 tensors distribute across ordinals 2–6 (412/368/368/368/293). So the V7 census is not merely internally consistent, it is *externally justified*: `shard_opens: 6`, `identity_hashes: 6`, `graph_payload_descriptor_leases: 5`, `identity_only_descriptors_retained: 0`, ordinal 1 as `IDENTITY_ONLY` with `graph_access_permitted: false` and graph ordinals `[2,3,4,5,6]`. The six shard sizes sum to exactly **238,458,632,928**, matching `identity_hash_bytes` and confirming `complete_file_required: true` covers all six including the identity-only shard.

**Other dimensions verified.** Pre/post descriptor stability over a 9-field identity record (dev, ino, mode, size, mtime_ns, ctime_ns, ordinal, filename, role) with `descriptor_stability` banked per shard receipt. Lease ownership by `PACKAGE_COORDINATOR` with `expected_closures: 5`, `live_leases_after_close: 0`, `duplicate_closures: 0`, `unknown_leases: 0`. Primary/secondary continuity via `same_underlying_file_identity_as_primary` + `stability_rechecked_after_primary`. Accounting: `authorization_mint_delta: 0`, `unstarted_consumer_delta: 0`, one durable start each for package/primary/secondary. Historical ledger 175 → 175, delta 0, corroborated across ~10 independent banked artifacts. Numerical isolation intact — the v3 numerical contract was last touched at `a49a906c` (before the freeze), is untouched by this commit, and its sha256 `84ff9ba0…` matches both pins. Path-timing obligations are mutually satisfiable under the stated ordering. Production no-access preparation holds: `active_live_generation` moved `V6 → NONE`, `implementation_phase_entered: false`, validator reports `original_checkpoint_access: 0`, and the freeze introduces no checkpoint I/O whatsoever. **All 8 authority manifest sha256 bindings verify**, and the committed validator and unit test pass.

## 2. Findings

### BLOCKING — none

No finding authorizes a descriptor leak, an unauthorized read, a ledger mutation, or an unsound identity binding. The safety invariant is stated absolutely in a pinned authority and applies to *every* outcome: `close_at_package_terminal: true`, `failure.close_all_live_descriptors: true`, `live_leases_after_close: 0`.

### NON_BLOCKING_REQUIRED — resolve before implementation entry

1. **`path-timing-v7.json` is non-canonical, and it is precisely the pinned authority the validator does not gate.** Keys are unsorted — `"schema"` precedes `"satisfiable"` at byte 866 (same length, order only), violating `F017_CANONICAL_JSON_BYTES_V1`. The validator's `load()` raises `noncanonical authority`, but covers only 6 of the 8 pinned authorities; `path_timing`, `artifact_schemas`, and `numerical_contract` are hash-checked yet never canonicality-checked. `path_timing` is the one that is actually broken. Any canonical rewrite changes its bytes, its sha256, and breaks the manifest binding.
2. **`outcome-obligations-v7.json` is an unbound authority** — present in the tree, but absent from the manifest's 8 pinned entries and never loaded by the validator. The document governing failure terminalization is itself unpinned and unverified.
3. **4 of 9 outcomes have no obligations**: `COMPARISON_FAILURE`, `EVIDENCE_BANKING_FAILURE`, `PACKAGE_PRE_START_FAILURE`, `PRE_MINT_FAILURE`. `COMPARISON_FAILURE` is the most exposed — it occurs with all 5 graph leases live, yet no obligation requires `descriptor_lease_terminal` or asserts `live_descriptor_count_at_terminal: 0` for it. Closure is behaviorally mandated by the continuity contract, so this is an *evidence* gap, not an open leak — but it is single-layer coverage on the most descriptor-exposed failure path.
4. **The lifecycle transition relation encodes only the happy path** — 26 states, 13 edges, all success. All five failure terminal states (`CHECKPOINT_IDENTITY_TERMINAL_FAILURE`, `PRIMARY_/SECONDARY_/COMPARISON_/PACKAGE_TERMINAL_FAILURE`) are fully isolated: zero inbound, zero outbound edges. No failure state reaches `DESCRIPTOR_LEASES_RELEASED`. Failure terminalization is asserted in prose fields but not expressed in the model. The 7 pre-handshake states are likewise isolated.
5. **Cross-authority ordering inconsistency at the security-relevant boundary.** The identity contract's `ordering` asserts `CHECKPOINT_IDENTITY_COMPLETE → PRIMARY_DURABLE_STARTED`; the lifecycle model interposes `DESCRIPTOR_LEASES_ACTIVE` — the exact state where the 5 graph leases become active. Six of seven adjacencies match; this one does not. The validator hardcodes the identity contract's version, so it enforces the sequence that omits lease activation.
6. **`strict_key_census: true` is claimed but nested structures have no census**: `leases`, `ordered_shards`, `shard_receipt_sha256s`, and `pre_hash_descriptor_identity` / `post_hash_descriptor_identity` are opaque. Relatedly, `lease_id` is required by `LEASE_ID_MATCH` and appears in access events and `closed_lease_ids`, but is **not** among the 9 `descriptor_identity_fields` — the lease object's shape is undefined.
7. **`stability_rechecked_after_primary: true` has no artifact schema.** None of the 8 defined artifacts captures the post-primary recheck, so the obligation produces no banked evidence.
8. **Ledger-entry-id with zero ledger delta.** `checkpoint_identity_durable_start` requires `identity_stage_ledger_entry_id`, while accounting sets `checkpoint_identity_stage_delta: 0`. Whether this references an existing package-attempt entry or implies an unaccounted increment is unspecified.
9. **Root-of-trust status and anchoring.** The manifest is `DESIGN_REVIEW_DRAFT` while the contracts it pins are `DESIGN_FROZEN_NOT_LIVE`, and nothing pins the manifest itself — the root of trust is unanchored.
10. **`required` lists are inconsistently composed.** All five defined outcomes carry `package_delta: 1`, but `package_durable_start` is required in only 2 of 5 (omitted from all three post-identity failure outcomes). `descriptor_lease_manifest` is required only for `COMPLETE_SUCCESS`, though leases were necessarily manifested in the primary/secondary failure paths too.

### DEFENSE_IN_DEPTH

- 4 of 9 v7 contracts carry no `status` field at all; the freeze posture is asserted by only 3.
- Stability is checked pre-hash, post-hash, and post-primary, but there is no monitoring *within* a consumer's own read window; an in-place write through another fd to the same inode is caught only at the next checkpoint. Inherent to the model and acceptable — worth an explicit residual-risk note.
- `path_timing` defines no entry for the identity state root or the `checkpoint_identity_durable_start` artifact, though it covers the manifest, lease manifest, and access journal.
- Consider extending the validator to assert outcome-obligation coverage over `model["outcomes"]` and transition-relation totality, which would have caught findings 3 and 4 mechanically.

## 3. Verdict

`ACCEPT_CHECKPOINT_IDENTITY_LIFECYCLE_V7_FOR_IMPLEMENTATION`

V7 correctly diagnoses and structurally closes the V6 defect. The fix is the right one: it does not add a check to detect substitution, it removes the second `open()` that made substitution expressible, and it revokes the external identity-injection channel. The census is not just self-consistent but independently justified by committed catalog evidence — the identity-only designation of ordinal 1 is provably correct, not stipulated. Numerical authority is isolated and untouched, the historical ledger is unmoved at 175, and the no-access posture is intact.

The ten `NON_BLOCKING_REQUIRED` findings are completeness and consistency defects concentrated in the *modeling and evidence* layer — an unpinned obligations document, a happy-path-only transition relation, uncovered failure outcomes, one non-canonical authority, and partial validator coverage. None of them authorizes unsafe behavior, because the descriptor-closure invariant is stated unconditionally in a pinned authority that applies to all outcomes. They should be resolved before implementation entry, and the manifest promoted from `DESIGN_REVIEW_DRAFT` to frozen with the obligations contract pinned and the validator's canonicality gate widened to all eight authorities.
